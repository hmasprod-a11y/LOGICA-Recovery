# Hanouty business operations: isolated preview boundary

This is a **new opt-in**, read-only business layer. It does **not** replace or invoke LOGICA's working purchase/stock workflows. No SQL, WinForms, MySQL connector, migration, stock mutation or license changes are included. The new catalogue validation is a snapshot check, not a transactional consistency guarantee.

- `PurchaseReceiptPreviewService`: validates a supplier receipt and aggregates quantities/cost by exact `ID_ARTICLE` identity (not barcode), preserving duplicate-barcode variants and fractional decimal values. A conflicting barcode for one ID fails closed.
- `StockCountVarianceService`: compares physical counts with recorded stock, separately reporting shortage and surplus without adjusting stock. Negative *recorded* stock is allowed because it may exist in legacy data; negative physical counts are rejected.
- `StockCountCoverageService`: compares a physical stocktake against an authoritative catalogue snapshot by exact article ID, reporting missing and unknown articles explicitly. An uncounted product is **never** treated as counted zero; `IsComplete` is false until every baseline ID is accounted for and no unknown ID exists. This is preview-only and does not authorize posting.
- `PurchaseReceiptCatalogueValidationService`: compares an already validated receipt against a caller-supplied authoritative catalogue snapshot by exact `ID_ARTICLE`, detecting unknown IDs and changed barcodes. Duplicate barcodes across different IDs are valid; duplicate catalogue IDs fail closed. This check is advisory until a reviewed integration owns the transaction and revalidation boundary.
- `StockCountAdjustmentProposalService`: produces read-only, non-zero adjustment deltas only when the full baseline is physically counted and no unknown articles remain. A proposal is not an authorization or database command.
- `PurchaseReceiptStockProjectionService`: combines an already validated receipt with caller-provided catalogue and stock snapshots to preview the exact per-article quantity after receiving goods. It rejects unknown/changed catalogue identities, missing or duplicate stock rows, and decimal overflow; duplicate barcodes remain distinct by `ID_ARTICLE`. It preserves legacy negative recorded quantities and fractional costs. This is **not** an authorization to post a purchase or a freshness/locking guarantee. The future MariaDB adapter must re-read and revalidate inside its own transaction.
- `PurchaseReceiptStockProjectionSmoke`: regression coverage for identity, stale catalogue, incomplete/duplicate stock snapshots, overflow, case sensitivity, read-only results and no stock writes.
- `StockCountFreshnessService`: compares a **complete** physical count's original recorded quantities against a later caller-supplied stock snapshot. It reports changed, missing and newly added `ID_ARTICLE` identities, including articles whose original count variance was zero (which an adjustment-only comparison would miss). Negative legacy quantities, exact ordinal identities and input ordering are preserved. A fresh result is only an observation; it is **not** a lock or permission to post. Re-run under a future owned MariaDB transaction.
- `PurchaseReceiptSupplierValidationService`: checks the receipt's exact supplier identity against a complete caller-supplied supplier directory. Unknown supplier yields `IsKnown=false`; duplicate or malformed directory rows fail closed. This intentionally does not invent supplier status or database schema, and it never changes the receipt or creates a supplier.
- `BusinessSnapshotFreshnessSmoke`: Windows/.NET Framework and standalone .NET 10 regression for stale stock including zero-delta articles, added/deleted identities, negative quantities, duplicate supplier IDs, unknown suppliers, case sensitivity and immutable results.
- All services return read-only snapshots. Nothing is committed. Integration with the real purchase UI/database requires a separate, reviewed design and isolated MariaDB tests. Re-read authoritative catalogue and stock inside the final posting transaction before committing; these previews cannot establish a lock or guarantee freshness.

- `PurchaseReceiptFreshnessService`: rechecks an existing receipt projection against fresh supplier, catalogue and stock snapshots, reporting missing supplier, missing articles, barcode drift, missing stock rows and changed stock quantities. It rejects malformed projections and duplicate snapshot identities, while allowing unrelated stock changes. This is diagnostic only, not a posting authorization; the final DB transaction must repeat the check.\n- `StockCountReconciliationPreviewService`: composes full stocktake freshness and adjustment proposal generation. A stale or incomplete count cannot produce an adjustment plan, including when a zero-variance article changed after counting. No stock writes.\n- `BusinessRevalidationSmoke`: isolated .NET Framework and .NET 10 regression for stale receipts, duplicate-barcode variants, forged projections, supplier removal, count drift, and read-only results.\n\nRegression gates:
- Windows/.NET Framework compiler: `powershell -File scripts/test-catalogue-core.ps1` (included in existing Domain characterization workflow).
- .NET 10: `dotnet run --project business/Hanouty.BusinessOperations.Smoke.csproj -c Release`.

## Stock movement journal reconciliation (Track B)

- `StockMovementAuditService.Compare` checks a caller-supplied baseline, signed movement journal and current-stock snapshot using exact ordinal `ID_ARTICLE` and unique movement identities. It calculates expected quantities and per-article drift, and explicitly reports missing current rows, unexpected current articles and movements referencing unknown articles. Negative legacy stock and fractional movements are preserved; arithmetic overflow, duplicate IDs and malformed rows fail closed. The output is immutable and never writes stock, cost, prices or the database.
- `StockMovementAuditSmoke` runs with both Windows independent Domain characterization and the standalone .NET 10 Business Operations smoke project. This is **not** an audit of real shop journals: the caller must provide consistent, isolated snapshots and a complete movement range. A future MariaDB adapter and transaction owner need separate review and integration tests before use for posting or compliance reporting.

## Purchase order / goods receipt reconciliation (Track B)

- `PurchaseOrderReceiptReconciliationService.Compare` compares a caller-supplied purchase order with an already validated goods receipt by exact ordinal `ID_ARTICLE`. It preserves duplicate barcodes across distinct articles and accepts barcode-free articles.
- Shows partial and excess deliveries, unreceived order lines, unexpected received articles, and signed extended-cost differences against the agreed unit cost. Repeated order lines for the same article aggregate only when barcode and agreed cost match.
- Validates the **entire** receipt snapshot (positive quantities, nonnegative costs, unique IDs and matching aggregate totals) to reject forged or stale input rather than silently accepting mismatches. Cost expectations exclude unexpected articles while actual receipt cost includes them, so these totals must not be interpreted as a balanced accounting journal.
- Pure, immutable, offline-first read-only results. No MariaDB, stock writes, price changes, supplier mutation, or posting permission. Actual purchase order tables and receiving transaction semantics require Track D/DB owner coordination and isolated MariaDB fixtures before any integration.
- `PurchaseOrderReceiptReconciliationSmoke` executes in the Windows independent Domain smoke and the standalone .NET 10 Business Operations project; shared .NET 10 workflow does **not** automatically run the standalone project until Track A/D approve wiring.

## Gross stock movement flow report (Track B, 2026-11-01)

- `StockMovementFlowReportService.Summarize` derives gross inbound and outbound movement quantities, signed net change, event counts and expected/actual closing quantities for each exact `ID_ARTICLE`, preserving the opening snapshot's order. It builds on `StockMovementAuditService` to reject duplicate journal identities, duplicate stock rows, malformed inputs and overflow, and to report unknown journal articles, missing/added closing rows and drift. Case-distinct IDs and negative legacy opening stock remain valid.
- **Important:** totals count only movements for articles present in the opening snapshot; unknown-article movements are excluded from known totals **and make `IsReconciled=false`**. Missing closing stock is not treated as zero. Gross totals can overflow even if net movements would cancel; this fails closed. A single-use journal is consumed once.
- `StockMovementFlowReportSmoke` runs in the existing independent Windows Domain suite and standalone .NET 10 Business Operations project. Report output is immutable, read-only and offline-first. This is not a timestamped accounting report or proof of journal completeness: the caller must supply an authoritative opening snapshot, complete movement range and closing snapshot. No MariaDB adapter, stock posting, cost valuation, schema, pricing, activation or store data changes.
